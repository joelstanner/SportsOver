INSTALL SPORTSOVER ON YOUR MAC
============================

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or newer. No Node.js, npm or Terminal is needed.
Choose the arm64 download for Apple Silicon (M-series chips), or the x64
download for an Intel Mac. Apple menu > About This Mac shows your chip.

1. Open the downloaded SportsOver .dmg file.
2. Drag SportsOver into the Applications folder shown in the disk image.
3. Eject the SportsOver disk image, then open SportsOver from Applications.

FIRST LAUNCH

This early release is not signed with an Apple Developer ID or notarized
by Apple. macOS will normally block the first launch because it cannot
verify the developer or check the app for malicious software.

If you trust the download from https://github.com/joelstanner/SportsOver:

1. Try opening SportsOver from Applications, then dismiss the warning.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll to the message about SportsOver and choose Open Anyway.
4. Confirm Open and authenticate if macOS asks.

This approves this app; you do not need to disable system-wide security.
An organization-managed Mac may prohibit this exception. If the warning
says the app is damaged or contains malware, stop and report the exact
message rather than trying to bypass it.

Apple's guidance: https://support.apple.com/102445

USING SPORTSOVER

Settings opens when the app starts. Closing Settings leaves SportsOver
running. Use the scoreboard icon in the menu bar to show Settings or choose
Quit SportsOver. Cmd+Shift+U recovers a hidden or locked banner.
Internet access is needed for live scores, team logos and catalog updates.

UPDATING

Quit SportsOver, download the newer installer, and drag the new app into
Applications. Choose Replace when Finder asks. Your preferences and team
selections remain in ~/Library/Application Support/SportsOver.
Updates are manual; this release does not update itself.

UNINSTALLING

Quit SportsOver, then move SportsOver from Applications to the Trash.
Saved preferences remain available if you reinstall later.
